==========================================
RELATIONSHIPS
==========================================
- RunTicketMiner uses DataManager
- RunTicketMiner delegates to OrganizerMenu, AdminMenu, CustomerMenu
- OrganizerMenu implements EventManageable
- AdminMenu implements EventManageable
- AdminMenu throws EventNotFoundException
- OrganizerMenu throws EventNotFoundException
- CustomerMenu throws EventNotFoundException
- OrganizerMenu, AdminMenu, CustomerMenu log actions via Logger
- DataManager stores Users, Events, and Venues
- RunTicketMiner, AdminMenu, OrganizerMenu use InputReader
- AdminMenu, OrganizerMenu, CustomerMenu use DataManager for resolveEvent
- AdminMenu uses DataManager for resolveUser, resolveVenue
